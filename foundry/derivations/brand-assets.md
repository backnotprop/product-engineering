# Derivation: brand-assets

On-brand standalone asset generation: SVG illustrations for articles and posts, repo
and social images, logo and identity work, on-brand feature concepts. The kit's one
mostly-new spine — written last, after the method was proven on the converged four,
with the anti-slop gates applied hardest (this file and the adversarial review are the
evidence).

## Why it exists

Nothing else in the kit generates standalone assets, and nothing in the source corpus
covers SVG authoring at all. It also replaces a recurring manual chore: pointing
agents at brand files every time — here the brand lookup is the skill's mandatory
first step.

## Spine

`skills/brand-assets/SKILL.md` — authored. The workflow: locate brand truth
(DESIGN.md, style guides, a `brand/` folder) and refuse to invent brand values →
derive the asset's rules from the tokens → author SVG-first with quality rules →
verify against the brand → report tokens and motifs used. Cross-references design
(DESIGN.md contract) and design·direct (anti-generic craft) instead of restating them.

## Imagegen mode: deliberately absent

The roster allowed an optional tool-gated imagegen mode. Per the standing ruling that
cut the imagegen medium, it is not built. If image generation ever enters the
environment, it arrives as one new distilled reference via the new-skill runbook —
nothing in the spine assumes or forbids it beyond stating SVG-in-code as the medium.

## Rulings applied

L-10 (motivated motion) applies trivially — static assets by default; animation in an
SVG asset only on explicit request, routed to build·motion for craft.
